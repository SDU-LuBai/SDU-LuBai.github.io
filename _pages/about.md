Lu Bai, Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at Shandong University, Taishan Scholar Young Scholar, and IEEE Senior Member. The primary research is focused on intelligent networks and connected intelligence. More than ten research projects have been undertaken, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China (General Program and Young Scientists Fund), the Shandong Provincial Natural Science Foundation (Excellent Young Scholars Program and General Program), the Open Research Project of the National Key Laboratory, and the National Defense Science and Technology Innovation Special Zone Project. Participation has also been made in over ten projects, such as the National High-Tech Research and Development Program of China, the National Science and Technology Major Project, the European Union Science and Technology Development Plan, and the UK Natural Science Foundation. Over fifty papers have been published in internationally renowned journals and conferences, including Nature sub-journals and IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials, with more than twenty as first author and over twenty as corresponding author. Two English monographs have been authored (one as first author), and seven national invention patents have been granted. Contributions have also been made to the drafting and formulation of eight industry standards, group standards, and white papers. Multiple honors have been received, including the Second Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award from the China Communications and Transportation Association, the Silver Award at the National Invention Exhibition, the IEEE VR Best Paper Award, the Third Prize of the Invention Entrepreneurship Award from the China Association of Inventions, the Innovation Achievement Award in the Field of Connected Vehicles from the China Institute of Communications, and the Outstanding Paper Award at the Aerospace Frontiers Conference.

In 2022, selection was made for the “Qilu Young Scholar” program. In 2023, approval was obtained for the Shandong Provincial Outstanding Youth Project, together with selection as a “Taishan Scholar Young Scholar” and inclusion in the 9th China Association for Science and Technology “Young Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program.” In 2024, selection was made for the “Xiaomi Young Scholar” program. Currently, positions are held as a member of the IEEE P1944 Standards Group, Vice Secretary-General of the Networked Intelligent Committee of the Chinese Association of Automation, editorial board member of multiple domestic and international journals, and program/technical committee member and sub-forum chair of several internationally renowned conferences.

Research Interests
======
- **Intelligent Networks**, refer to AI-enhanced 6G wireless communications. It specifically focuses on how AI algorithms complement and advance the theoretical framework of channel modeling methods, as well as on channel measurement and modeling in space–air–ground integrated 6G networks.
- **Networked Intelligence**, refers to multi-agent collaborative architectures, focusing on the synergistic integration of wireless communications and intelligent applications. The research explores how networked intelligence enables cooperative positioning, sensing, cognition, and decision-making among multiple agents.
